Camera Roll Cleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 (contact form and trial reminder added)

The short version

The Camera Roll Cleaner app collects no data. Your photos and videos are looked at on your iPhone and never leave it. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only time anything reaches us is if you choose to write to us through the contact form on the support page (see "Contact form" below).

Your photos and videos

With your permission, the app reads your photo library through Apple's Photos framework so it can show your photos and videos sorted by file size. All of this happens on your iPhone. The app does not upload, copy or send your photos, videos or any information about them anywhere.

When you delete items from the app's bin, iOS asks you to confirm, then moves them to Recently Deleted in the Photos app. The app cannot empty Recently Deleted; only you can.

What is stored on your iPhone

To work, the app keeps a few small things on your device only:

  • A size cache (sizes.json): the file size of each photo and video, keyed by Apple's identifier for the item, so the next launch does not have to measure everything again. It is kept in the app's cache folder and is not included in backups.
  • Your kept list, your bin list and your chosen order: the identifiers of the items you swiped right on (kept) and left on (bin), and whether you sort by size, month or date, so your choices survive closing the app.
  • Two numbers in the Keychain: how many deletes you have done with the app (the first 3 are free), and the total size of what you deleted, which the app shows after a delete. Because they are stored in the iOS Keychain, they stay on your iPhone even if you delete and reinstall the app. They are marked "this device only", so they are not moved to another iPhone.

None of this is sent off your device. Deleting the app removes everything except those two Keychain numbers.

Purchases

Swiping is free, and you get 3 free deletes, any size. After that, Camera Roll Cleaner Pro is available as a yearly subscription with a free trial or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases, trials, renewals and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details. It only asks Apple whether this Apple ID has an active Pro purchase.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only connections are those made by Apple's own frameworks, for example the App Store when loading prices or processing a purchase, and iCloud Photos when an item shown on screen is stored in iCloud.

Trial reminder

If you start the free trial, iOS asks whether the app may send you notifications. If you allow it, the app schedules one local reminder on your iPhone for 2 days before the trial ends. It is created and shown by your iPhone; nothing is sent to us. If you say no, nothing is scheduled.

Contact form

The support page has a contact form. If you choose to use it, the email address and message you type are sent through Formspree, the service that delivers the form to us. We use them only to answer you. The app itself never sends anything to the form or to us. To have a message deleted sooner, ask through the same form.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the contact form on the support page.
